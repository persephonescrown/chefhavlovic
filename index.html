Philosophy
“Technique is the discipline.Ingredients are the point.Everything on the plate earns its place.”

Andrew Havlovic's cooking is built on precision plating and confident, seasonal flavor combinations — a style shaped by more than a decade running kitchens across the country and abroad before returning home to Iowa. Every dish is composed with the same standard he now asks of his students: clean technique, honest ingredients, and nothing on the plate without a reason.

The Journey

From line cook to instructor

2006

DMACC Culinary Arts Graduate

Graduated from the Des Moines Area Community College Culinary Arts program and became the first-ever recipient of the Marge Anderson French Chef Exchange Scholarship, funding a formative stage in France.

2006 – 2023

Executive Chef, coast to coast and beyond

Led kitchens as Executive Chef across Minnesota, Maryland, Arizona, and the British Virgin Islands — from refined tasting menus to high-volume resort dining.

Teaching & Mentorship

Shaping the next generation

Since returning to the Iowa Culinary Institute in 2024, Chef Havlovic teaches and mentors student chefs in the same program he graduated from in 2006 — hands-on kitchen sessions, guest-dinner preparations, and technique instruction rooted in two decades of professional experience.

His classroom philosophy mirrors his kitchen standard: precision first, then flavor, then the confidence to make it your own.
